# Academic Terms of Use

DolyNation provides an AI-assisted adaptive reading environment designed to strengthen reading comprehension and contextual vocabulary across multiple target languages.

## 1. Account, access, and verification
Registration requires a valid email address confirmed through a signed verification link before selecting a subscription plan. Your credentials are personal; you are responsible for safeguarding your password and signing out on shared devices.

## 2. Subscription plans and study quotas
Each plan (Dolly, Dolly Plus, and Dolly Pro) defines daily AI reading generation quotas, contextual explanation limits, and the number of active target study languages. Upgrading or downgrading adjusts active slots and daily limits immediately.

## 3. Ethical use of AI and study materials
Generated readings, comprehension evaluations, and lexical explanations are intended for personal study and academic practice. Automated scraping, bypassing quota limits, or submitting abusive or unlawful prompts through custom categories or summaries is prohibited.

## 4. Platform updates
DolyNation may refine its linguistic models, topic moderation filters, and service terms periodically to preserve academic quality and system reliability.
