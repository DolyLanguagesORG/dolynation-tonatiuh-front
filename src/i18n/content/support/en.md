# Academic & Technical Support

Quick reference for resolving sign-in issues, email verification, study language configuration, and reading generation in DolyNation.

## 1. Missing confirmation or password reset email
Check your spam or promotions folder and confirm your email spelling. You can request a fresh signed link from `/verify-email` or `/forgot-password` once the 30-second cooldown completes.

## 2. Unable to generate new AI readings
To create adapted texts, your account needs an active subscription plan and at least 3 topic categories configured in `/setup-preferences` or in the preferences section of `/profile`.

## 3. Reading & glossary immersion vs. UI language
The language selector in the top header controls the outer platform interface (sidebar, navigation, forms, and profile). Each reading, evaluation flow, and glossary card remains immersed in the language of the reading item itself.

## 4. Signing out across all devices
If you signed in on a shared device, visit `/profile#security` and trigger global sign-out to revoke all active sessions immediately.
