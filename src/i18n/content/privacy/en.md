# Privacy Policy and Data Ethics

We safeguard your identity, reading archive, and linguistic progress through encrypted sessions and transparent controls over your account data.

## 1. Sessions and essential cookies
Authentication relies on protected `HttpOnly` cookies and rotating refresh tokens. We also store strictly functional preferences (such as your chosen interface language) in a preference cookie so your selection persists across visits.

## 2. Reading archive and personal glossary
We store your generated readings, comprehension evaluations, daily streak history, and saved glossary terms solely to support continuity across your study sessions.

## 3. Processing with AI models
Selected text passages in the reader and written comprehension summaries are processed exclusively to generate contextual explanations and educational feedback tailored to your study language.

## 4. Active session control
From your profile settings, you can revoke all active sessions across every signed-in device immediately whenever you wish to secure your account.
